# Skill: Adobe Stock Compliance

Compliance layer must check:
- originality
- meaningful differentiation
- commercial usefulness
- metadata accuracy
- content type accuracy
- AI disclosure requirements
- copyright/IP concerns
- spam risk
- near-duplicate risk

Important:
Adobe policies can change. For current submission rules, verify official Adobe Stock documentation before making a definitive current-policy decision.

The system should never claim that an asset is Adobe-approved. It can only classify it as passing the system's compliance checks or needing review.
